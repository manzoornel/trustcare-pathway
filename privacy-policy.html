Privacy Policy

Doctor Uncle Family Clinic (“Doctor Uncle”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and protect personal information through our website, healthcare services, recruitment forms, Meta lead forms and communications.

1. Information we collect

  • Name, phone number, email and address.
  • Appointment, enquiry and patient-portal information.
  • Health or medical information needed to provide clinical care.
  • Recruitment information such as experience, skills, CV, portfolio, LinkedIn profile, work location and answers submitted through our website or Facebook/Instagram instant forms.
  • Technical data such as browser, device, IP address, pages visited, analytics and cookie data.
  • Communications through phone, email, WhatsApp, Messenger or Instagram.

2. How we use information

  • To arrange appointments and provide healthcare services.
  • To respond to enquiries and send service communications.
  • To assess job applications and contact shortlisted candidates.
  • To operate, secure and improve our website and services.
  • To measure advertising performance and prevent spam or misuse.
  • To meet legal, regulatory and clinical-record obligations.

3. Meta lead forms

When you submit a Facebook or Instagram instant form, Meta may prefill information from your account and provide your responses to us. Recruitment responses are used only to evaluate applications, verify contact details and communicate about the vacancy. Meta also processes data under its own privacy policy. Submitting a form does not guarantee employment.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share necessary information with authorised clinic personnel, healthcare professionals, hosting and technology providers, analytics and communication platforms, recruitment providers, insurers or authorities when required by law.

5. Cookies and analytics

Our website may use essential cookies and analytics tools, including Google Analytics, to understand usage and improve performance. Browser settings can restrict non-essential cookies.

6. Retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably needed or legally required. Unsuccessful recruitment applications are deleted or anonymised when no longer required. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet system is completely secure.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access, correct or delete personal information, withdraw consent or object to certain processing. We may verify your identity. Clinical records may be retained where healthcare or legal requirements apply.

8. Children

A parent or legal guardian should provide information for a minor unless applicable law permits otherwise. Recruitment forms are intended for people legally eligible to work.

9. Updates

We may update this policy when our practices, services or legal obligations change. The latest version will appear on this page.
